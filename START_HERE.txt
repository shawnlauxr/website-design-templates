WEBSITE SHOWROOM — OFFLINE CLIENT DEMOS
====================================

IMPORTANT: DO NOT OPEN THE HTML FILE INSIDE THE ZIP PREVIEW.

STEP 1: Right-click website_showroom_FIXED.zip, then choose Extract All.
STEP 2: Open the extracted website_showroom_FIXED folder.
STEP 3: Double-click index.html (it opens in Chrome or Edge).
STEP 4: Click 'Open full-page demo' under any of the 10 styles.
STEP 5: Click 'All 10 designs' to go back; use 'Hosting explained' for costs.

All files must STAY IN THIS FOLDER TOGETHER. Do not move index.html
out by itself: the gallery links to the other local .html files.

No internet, hosting, paid account, SQL database or domain required.
Forms/buttons in these demonstrations are conceptual and do not store data.
All sample business content is fictional. Running costs are rough planning
estimates in SGD, not fees charged by the website developer.
