Simple guide to website running costs

What does it cost to keep a website online?

Think of a website like a shop. The domain is its address. Hosting is the space where it lives. A database is a filing cabinet that saves customer information. Not every website needs one.

Common questions

Can a website be free?

For a demonstration, yes. Some simple live websites can also use free hosting; a custom domain normally has an annual renewal cost.

Does every website need a database?

No. A portfolio or company information site can work without one.

Will I be locked into monthly developer fees?

No. Hosting accounts can belong to you, and you can manage ongoing costs directly with the providers.
